PUTTING THE APP ONLINE FOR IPHONE (free, about 5 minutes, done once by you)

1. Go to github.com and sign in (or create a free account).
2. Tap + > New repository. Name it: inspector-guide. Choose Public. Create.
3. Tap "uploading an existing file". Upload ALL files in this folder (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and the three icon files). Tap Commit.
4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under Branch choose main, folder /(root). Save.
5. After 1 to 2 minutes the page shows your link:  https://YOURNAME.github.io/inspector-guide/
6. Send that link to students by WhatsApp.

ON THE IPHONE (students do this once, with internet)
1. Open the link in SAFARI (not inside WhatsApp).
2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow).
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
4. Open "Inspector Guide" from the home screen. It now works offline.
